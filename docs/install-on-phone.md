# Try CozyMoon before publishing

You can test CozyMoon in two ways. Neither one puts it on Google Play.

## Option A: web preview (easiest, any phone or computer)

Open **https://missluckychen1991-source.github.io/cozymoon/** in your browser.
On a phone, you can add it to your home screen: in Chrome tap ⋮ → *Add to
Home screen*; on iPhone Safari tap Share → *Add to Home Screen*.

Good to know about the web preview:
- Your nickname, check-ins and Tea Table posts are saved in that browser only.
- The friend chat, the private Tea Table answer and the story maker use their
  friendly offline replies, because the web preview doesn't connect to
  Firebase.
- Vibration doesn't work in a browser, and the calm videos open YouTube in a
  new tab.

It updates by itself a few minutes after every change to the app.

## Option B: install the real app on an Android phone

No cables or developer tools needed.

1. On your Android phone, open
   **https://github.com/missluckychen1991-source/cozymoon/releases/tag/test-build**
2. Under *Assets*, tap **cozymoon-test.apk** to download it.
3. When the download finishes, tap the file in the notification (or open it
   from the *Files* / *Downloads* app).
4. Android will say it can't install apps from this source. Tap
   **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source** for your browser, then go
   back and tap **Install**.
5. If Google Play Protect warns that it doesn't recognise the app, tap
   **More details → Install anyway**. That warning appears for any app that
   isn't from the Play Store.
6. Open **CozyMoon** from your home screen. 🎉

To update later, repeat steps 1 to 3 and tap **Install** again. Your data is
kept.

You can send the same link to friends with Android phones so they can test
too.
